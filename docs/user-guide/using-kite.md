# Using Kite (version 1.1.0)

Kite listens to your call, shows a live transcript, and writes a short answer you can say when you press a key. It runs in the **desktop app** (Windows) or in **Chrome or Edge**. To install the desktop app, see [Install Kite on Windows](install-windows.md).

## Use it where AI assistance is allowed

Use Kite in calls where AI assistance is allowed — your own meetings, client calls, practice and mock interviews. Don't use it to get hidden help in interviews, exams or assessments where outside help isn't permitted. Screen-share protection only hides Kite's windows; it does nothing against monitoring or proctoring tools. Recording or transcribing others may require their consent.

## Between calls: the home panel (desktop app)

The desktop app opens as a small panel: your sessions, newest first, with a search box and a sort button. Each card shows the date, title, description, type, whether the transcript is saved, and its status. **Start** begins a call; **View transcript** opens an ended one.

- **View in dashboard** and **Create session** open the full pages in a larger window.
- **⋮** has your account, **Dashboard**, **Private** (see below), **Zoom**, **Theme** (Light, Dark or System) and **Sign out**.
- Drag the header to move the panel, and any edge to resize it. Kite remembers where you left it.

## Before a call: create a session

1. On the dashboard, choose **New session**.
2. **Details:** the call type, the company or topic, your role, the other person's name if you like (it shows on their lines), a short description, and the documents Kite should use (a CV, a price list, a runbook). Add **key terms** (names, product words) so the transcript spells them right; Kite also recognises spoken numbers, such as "BM twenty five" for BM25.
3. **Preferences:** answer format, length (Short ≈ 10–20 s of speech, Balanced ≈ 20–40 s, Detailed ≈ 40–60 s), tone and language. You can change your defaults for new sessions in **Settings**.

Your sessions, documents, transcripts and answers are stored **on this computer only**. Audio never leaves it.

## During a call

1. Open the session and choose **Connect**. Kite listens on two channels: **You** (your microphone) and **Them** (the other person).
   - **Desktop app:** Kite hears the other person through system audio; there is no picker.
   - **Browser:** share the tab the call is in when Chrome or Edge asks. Turn on **Also share tab audio**.
2. Choose **Go live**. The transcript fills in, oldest at the top and newest at the bottom.
3. When they ask a question, press **Answer** (**Ctrl+Enter**). The answer streams in: one line you can say as it is, 2 to 4 short points, and sometimes a **Tip**.

Kite answers real questions. If the latest words are a greeting or "say yes when you're ready", it goes back to the last real question. If the words were noise, it says so: press **Answer** again, or type the question. To answer a greeting anyway, press **Answer this** on that bubble.

### The bars (desktop app)

During a call the desktop app is a stack of slim bars over your screen, with panels that open under them.

**Bar 1:**
- **Sound and microphone status:** a red dot means Kite is capturing. **▾** chooses the microphone.
- **Answer** (**Ctrl+Enter**), **Screenshot** (**Ctrl+Shift+Enter**) and **Chat** (**Ctrl+Shift+Space**).
- Drag it to move the bars. While you drag, three places light up along the top of the screen: drop on one to go there. **Ctrl+Alt+Arrow** nudges them, **Ctrl+Alt+H** shows or hides them, and each screen remembers its own place.
- Collapse, **⋮**, and the timer, which is also **End**: press it and confirm.

**Bar 2** shows sound levels and the latest words, or the question they are asking, highlighted. **Clear** (**Ctrl+Shift+Backspace**) clears the words on screen. **⤢** opens the transcript panel.

### The transcript panel

Each turn is a bubble: you on the right, the other person on the left, with their name (or "Client 1") and the time underneath. It follows the newest line; scroll up to read and it stops, with **Jump to latest** to go back. Questions show **Answer this**. Lines that are only noise collapse into **Unclear speech**: press it to see them. The panel's header has the **Language**, **Clear** and collapse.

### The answer panel

It opens by itself when an answer starts, with the question at once and **Thinking…** until the words arrive. **Stop** ends an answer early.

- **Question:** what Kite answered, with a copy button.
- **Answer:** the line in bold, then the points. A figure Kite could not find in your documents is underlined: check it before you say it.
- The footer shows the question type, the time, 👍 / 👎 and report. Ratings help Kite learn your preferences (see **Settings → What Kite has learned**).
- **← / →** (**Ctrl+Left / Ctrl+Right**) move between this call's questions. **Clear** (**Ctrl+Backspace**) goes back to the newest answer; it never empties the card.
- A question asked twice within a minute shows once, with the newest answer.

Both panels can be open together, the transcript above the answer. Kite remembers which were open. They are see-through; **⋮ → Opacity** sets how much (40–100 %, 70 % by default). The text always stays solid. Over a bright page, a lower value makes text harder to read.

### The ⋮ menu during a call

**Dashboard** and **Edit session** (in their own window, so the call keeps going), **Private**, **Zoom**, **Theme**, **Opacity**, **Font size**, **Language**, **Auto answer**, **Open call window** (the full view, for when you want everything large), **Reset position**, and how answers are written.

### Coding questions

From a screenshot or a typed question: the **approach** in one line, **Key steps**, **code** in your coding language with its own copy button, **Complexity**, and one line on how to explain it.

### Typing a question or sending a screenshot

- **Chat** (**Ctrl+Shift+Space**): a box opens in bar 1. Type, or speak with the microphone, and press **Enter**. **Auto** decides for you, **For the call** gives an answer you can say, and **Ask Kite** gives a private answer for you only.
- **Screenshot** (**Ctrl+Shift+Enter**): Kite works out what is on your screen and answers to suit, together with what they just asked:
  - a coding problem: as above;
  - code with an error: the cause, the fix and corrected code;
  - a slide or document: its key points and how to respond;
  - a table or chart: the takeaway and key numbers;
  - something it cannot read: it says so.

### The browser view

During a call the browser view fills the whole window; the sidebar comes back when the call ends. It shows the call side by side from 1280 px wide, and stacked on smaller screens:

- **Left, top:** a large live preview of the shared tab, with **Change tab** (share another tab without ending the call) and **Minimize** (open the overlay) in its corner.
- **Left, under the preview:** **Stop**, **Clear**, the audio levels and the answer **Language**, then the transcript in bubbles.
- **Right, top:** the session and its status, the call timer, the **⋮** menu (**Theme**, **Larger text**, **Smaller text**, **Auto answer**, **Edit session** in a new tab, answer preferences, **Clear answers**) and **End**.
- **Right:** your answers as cards, newest at the top, the message box with its microphone, and the big **Answer** and **Screenshot** buttons. The arrow on the window's right edge hides or shows this side.
- Drag the divider to resize the two sides. Kite remembers where you left it.
- The shortcuts work while Kite's tab has focus; only the desktop app has them everywhere.

### Private

**Private** (in each **⋮** menu and in Settings) leaves **all of Kite's windows** out of screen shares: the bars, panels, call window, home panel and Ask Kite. It is one setting, off by default. Windows 10 version 2004 or later; on macOS, some capture apps may still show them. Kite stays in your taskbar, and it does nothing against monitoring or proctoring tools.

### If nobody seems to be there

After 10 minutes with nothing from the other person and no answers, Kite asks **Still in a call?**. After 30, it pauses listening, with a **Resume** button. If your speakers keep repeating the other person into your microphone, Kite hides those lines and suggests headphones, once.

### If the transcript stops

If sound arrives but no words appear for 8 seconds, Kite restarts speech recognition by itself and shows **Transcription restarted**. The call and its transcript continue.

## After a call

Choose **End**. Kite saves the transcript (if **Save transcript** is on for the session) and can write **call notes**. Open the session to read them.

The **Transcript** section has two tabs. **Conversation** is the call as a chat, with the speaker above each turn and a **Kite answered ▸** chip that opens each answer where it was asked. **Answers** lists every answer as a card. A session with several calls shows a divider between them. **Download** gives the conversation as TXT or PDF.

## Ask Kite

**Ask Kite** (**Ctrl+Alt+K**) is a private assistant for anything: general questions, maths, code. Its answers are for you, not for the call, and are kept on this computer.

## Settings

- **Accurate transcription:** **Auto** (the default) uses it when plugged in and not on battery, once its 661 MB model is downloaded; Kite never starts that download by itself. **On** and **Off** are there too.
- **Defaults for new sessions**, **Answers** (format, length, tone, model, coding language, what to do when your CV has no example, your own AI instructions).
- **Overlay:** Private, opacity and how far it fades away from the pointer.
- **Hotkeys:** see and check every shortcut. On keyboards where Ctrl+Alt types a letter (AltGr), Kite tells you if a shortcut clashes.
- **What Kite has learned:** the lessons Kite has picked up from your ratings; remove any you do not want.
- **Export my data** and **Local data** (delete everything Kite stored on this computer).
- **Administrators only:** **Answers → Answer style version.** **v-current** (the default) keeps your real CV projects exactly as the CV states them and gives extra depth as your approach. **v-previous** is the earlier answer style, kept so it can be switched back if needed.

## Honest answers

Kite answers from your session details, documents and the call. It does not invent prices, personal numbers or results. When something is not in your documents, it gives your approach or says you will confirm after the call, and a private **Note:** line tells you what to check. Always read an answer before you say it.
