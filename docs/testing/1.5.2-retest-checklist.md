# 1.5.2 re-test: 15 minutes with Claude voice

Re-test of ADR-0070 (the right question at the right time, echo cancelling, recordings, the Private warning). Same set-up as the call of 11 Nov 2026 (14:31): you on the laptop, Claude voice as the prospect, the 14-question discovery script, recording on, reading each answer aloud. Numbers only: never share the transcript or the answers.

## Before the call (2 minutes)

1. Paramount AI **1.5.2** (⋮ → About). It updates by itself; if not, close and open it once.
2. Settings → Transcription: **Accurate on**. Settings → Recording: **Standard 720p**. Auto answer **on**.
3. The large-documents session (about 470k characters), as on 11 Nov.
4. Claude voice on the **laptop speakers** for the first half, **headphones** for the second half (the echo canceller is checked both ways).

## The call (about 10 minutes)

5. Connect, then **Record → Screen + audio** (tick the consent). Private comes on by itself.
6. Have Claude voice ask the 14 questions with normal pauses, in this order of triggers:
   - Questions 1–7: press **Ctrl+Enter** — for some, *while the question is still being asked* (the grey line), for some right as it ends, and once as it only says "Next question…" (you should see "Listening for the question…", then the card).
   - Questions 8–14: no key. Let auto-answer answer.
   - Read each answer aloud as soon as it appears, and keep reading while the next question starts on a few of them.
   - Once: have it say "Sorry, can you repeat that last part?". No card should come; press Ctrl+Enter on it once: the previous card shows again (no new card).
   - Once: have it ask an earlier question again a few minutes later (for example the cloud platforms one): it gets a card again.
7. In the middle, turn **Private off** in ⋮: the overlay and the call window say "Private is off: Paramount AI will appear in your recording" and the Rec pill says "Visible in recording". Press **Turn Private back on**: both go away.
8. Watch for, and note the time of anything odd:
   - a card for the previous question, a second card for one question, a card for "repeat that" or a greeting;
   - a question with no card (auto-answer half);
   - the other side's words under your name;
   - a grey line that takes more than about a second to show.

## After the call (3 minutes)

9. End. Settings → Transcription → **Download call timing log** (ids and times only). Its `summary.cards` lists every card: trigger, question end → sent → first word → done; `call.echoCancel` should say `on`.
10. Review → the session → **Download transcript (TXT)**.
11. Open the recording (Meetings → Play): it should sound clean with no crackle on loud moments.

## What to send back

- The **call timing log** (JSON) and the **TXT** — both from this call.
- One line per odd moment you noted (its time and what you saw).

## What good looks like

| Measure (from the timing log) | Target |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+Enter → first word, p50 | ≤ 1.5 s (≤ 2 s with the large documents) |
| Question end → auto first word, p50 / p95 | ≤ 3 s / ≤ 4 s |
| Presses answering the right question | all |
| Cards for meta or small talk / duplicate cards | 0 / 0 |
| Them speech → first grey words, p50 / p95 | ≤ 1 s / ≤ 2 s |
| `echoCancel` | `on` |
