# 1.5.1 re-test: the 20-question demo with a team member

Re-test of the call-latency fixes (ADR-0069) on a real call. About 30 minutes. Numbers only: never share the transcript or the answers.

## Before the call

1. On the laptop under test: Paramount AI **1.5.1** (⋮ → About, or Settings), plugged in. Check it is found in **Start → search "Paramount AI"**.
2. Settings → Transcription: **Accurate on** (the default). Settings → Recording: **Standard 720p**.
3. One session, **CV only** (as on 11 Nov). A second session with **~470k characters** of documents for step 9.
4. A team member on Google Meet (or Zoom) as the other side, with the 20 questions. The tester uses headphones.
5. Leave Chrome with the meeting open, as in a real call. No other heavy apps.

## During the call (CV-only session)

6. Turn on **Record → Screen + audio** (tick the consent).
7. The team member asks the 20 questions, with normal pauses. Mix it up:
   - Questions 1–10: the tester presses **Ctrl+Enter** as soon as each question ends, and starts saying "So, um…" over the end of a few of them.
   - Questions 11–20: no key. Let auto-answer answer.
   - Somewhere in between: "Hi, uh, how are you doing?", "Can you hear me okay?" and one remark ("Let me pull up my notes").
8. Watch for, and note the time of anything odd:
   - a grey line showing the other person's words within about a second;
   - the answer card starting within about 2 s of Ctrl+Enter;
   - any "previous question" answer, duplicate card or card for small talk;
   - the notices: "Your lines will be filled in after the call", "Catching up…", "We can't hear…".
9. End the call. Repeat steps 6–8 with **5 questions** on the large-document session.

## After the call

10. Settings → Transcription → **Download call timing log**. Send the file (times and ids only) to the developer.
11. Review → the session: every call has a divider; nothing before the first call's divider.
12. Mic check (one minute): mute the microphone in Windows during a call and let the other side talk: within about 15 s "We can't hear your microphone — check the 🎤 picker" appears, and its button opens the microphone list. Unmute: it goes away.
