**Paramount AI 1.7.5 for Windows**

- **Check for Updates…** in the tray menu (right-click the Paramount AI icon): checks now and says "You're up to date", or downloads the new version and asks to restart (never during a call).
- **Show or hide the bars is now Ctrl+Alt+Y.** Ctrl+Alt+H was Google Meet's "raise hand", so pressing it during a Meet call raised your hand.
- **Turn click-through off** in the tray menu during a call.
- **Screenshots take the screen under your mouse pointer.** With more than one screen, the answer card says which one ("Screen 2") and offers **Wrong screen?**, which answers again about the next screen. The small picture stays in memory only.
- Upgrading keeps your sessions, settings, sign-in, recordings, Drive uploads and Private setting.

Install: download `Paramount-AI-Setup-1.7.5.exe` below, or use **Download for Windows** in the app. Windows may warn that the app is unrecognised: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Installed apps update by themselves.
