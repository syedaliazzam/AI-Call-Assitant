**Paramount AI 1.7.6 for Windows**

- **No pop-ups after a call.** Paramount AI no longer shows Windows notifications or a restart question on its own, because people in your meeting could see them if you are still sharing your screen.
  - Each session's card shows its Google Drive upload: **Uploading…**, **Saved to Drive ✓** or **Upload failed — Retry**.
  - Updates install the next time you quit Paramount AI. To update sooner, right-click the tray icon and choose **Check for Updates…**.
- Upgrading keeps your sessions, settings, sign-in, recordings, Drive uploads and Private setting.

Install: download `Paramount-AI-Setup-1.7.6.exe` below, or use **Download for Windows** in the app. Windows may warn that the app is unrecognised: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Installed apps update by themselves.
